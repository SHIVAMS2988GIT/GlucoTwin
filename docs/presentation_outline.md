# Presentation Outline — GlucoTwin

Suggested 10-slide deck for a 20-minute demo.

1. **Title:** GlucoTwin — A Synthetic Digital Twin for Glucose-Spike Risk
2. **Problem:** Why reactive monitoring can miss changing patterns; carefully distinguish motivation from claims.
3. **Challenge fit:** EHR + wearable data + adverse-event prediction + doctor-facing UI.
4. **Use case:** Illustrative two-hour glucose-spike risk scenario.
5. **Data strategy:** Synthetic EHR profiles and five-minute wearable-style time series.
6. **Architecture:** Data generation, feature engineering, model, FastAPI, React.
7. **ML approach:** Random Forest, target construction, train/test setup, metrics.
8. **Live product demo:** Patient selection, trend chart, scenario controls, prediction response.
9. **Limitations and responsible AI:** Synthetic data, no clinical validation, subgroup risks, privacy.
10. **Roadmap and conclusion:** Patient-level split, external validation, calibration, explainability, clinician review.

Use clear labels for all synthetic data and include the non-clinical disclaimer on the model/demo slides.
